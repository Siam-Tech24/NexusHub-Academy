Student Help & Guide

Everything you need to know about enrollment, classes, and payments.

How to Enroll in a Course

1

Choose Your Course

Go to Courses and pick the course you want to join.

2

Submit Your Registration

Click Enroll Now, fill out the application form with your details, and submit it to save your request in our database.

3

WhatsApp Confirmation

Once your request is reviewed and approved, you'll receive a confirmation message on WhatsApp. Confirm your request to proceed.

4

Batch Assignment

After confirmation, you will be successfully added to your course batch WhatsApp Group.

How to Attend Classes

Download the Zoom app on your device. All live interactive lessons will be hosted on Zoom, and meeting links will be shared directly in your WhatsApp batch group. Simply click the link to join your daily lessons. Important class notices and notes will also be posted there.

Payment Guidelines

Every student enjoys a 1-week free trial before any payment is required. After the trial week, you can choose whether or not to continue with the full course. Payments can easily be made via bKash (if you are joining from Bangladesh) or sent directly to the instructor's bank account (if you are joining internationally).

Need More Help?

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please reach out to us through our Contact Page. We are here to help you with any issues or concerns you may have regarding enrollment, classes, or payments.
