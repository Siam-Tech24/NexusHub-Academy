How to enroll in a course in NexusHub Academy

First Step

Go to Courses and choose a course

Click Enroll, fill up the form and click submit - Your request will be saved in the database

Next step

After your request is approved, you'll get a confirmation message in WhatsApp. Confirm your request

Next step

After your request is confirmed, you will be added to your course batch in WhatsApp Group.

How to attend the classes

Download Zoom app. All the classes will be held in zoom and the meeting link will be sent in the WhatsApp Group. Click it and join your everyday lesson. Important note will also be shared in the WhatsApp group

How to do the Payments

Every student will get 1 week free lessons before payment. After those 1 week lesson, you can choose to whether join the full course or not. You can do the payment using bKash(If from Bangladesh) or send it in the instructors bank account(If not from Bangladesh)
